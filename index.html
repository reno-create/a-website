ABOUT AEARIS

Curiosity drives discovery. Engineering makes it possible.

Aearis Oy is a Finnish technology venture exploring the intersection of research, design and engineering.

Our interests include unmanned aircraft systems, aerospace technologies, intelligent systems and emerging engineering applications.

We approach technology through independent learning, technical research, experimentation and the development of practical concepts.

Our objective is to build technical knowledge, explore new possibilities and contribute to the understanding of evolving technologies.

RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Exploring technology. Understanding possibilities.

Unmanned Aircraft Systems

Engineering & Design
